How a group deal works

No coupon codes and no haggling. The more people in the group, the lower the price.

  1. Pick a deal

    Browse electronics, fashion and more. Every product shows the group price next to the individual price.

  2. Invite your friends

    Share the group on WhatsApp or copy the link. Friends join in one tap.

  3. Fill the group

    When the last person joins, the group is created and everyone gets the group price.

Questions

The short answers.

What is a group deal?

A deal with two prices: the individual price and a lower group price. When enough people join the group, everyone gets the lower price.

What if the group doesn’t fill up?

The details are still being designed. Early-access members will see exactly how it works before they join their first group.

Where and when does it launch?

Bengaluru first, on Android, then across India.

Do my friends need the app?

Friends get an invite on WhatsApp or a copy link and join the group from there.
